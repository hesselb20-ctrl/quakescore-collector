Before a night

  • Hard surface, phone flat, face up or down. Not on a bed or sofa.
  • Keep this page open and in front. If the phone locks or you switch app, the session ends and what was measured so far is kept.
  • On iPhone, set Auto-Lock to Never for the night if the screen still turns off.
  • Upper floors show more than lower ones.

This is a field-test tool. Browsers deliver about 60 sensor readings per second with coarse timing, which is less than the app gets. One night usually shows the phone's own noise level and rarely a building mode. It is a screening measurement, not an engineering assessment.
